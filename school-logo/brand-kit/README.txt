01-logos
  svg/              Vector files; use these for print, signage, and any enlargement.
  png/              High-resolution images on a white background.
  png-transparent/  High-resolution images with no background, for placing over colour or photos.

  emblem                      The arch emblem on its own (profile pictures, IDs, merchandise).
  lockup-stacked              Primary logo (tarpaulins, certificates, covers).
  lockup-stacked-tagline      Primary logo with tagline.
  lockup-horizontal           Secondary logo (letterheads, website headers, signage).
  lockup-horizontal-tagline   Secondary logo with tagline.
  lockup-horizontal-reversed  Secondary logo for dark backgrounds (shirts, banners).
  seal                        Official seal, dark version.
  seal-light                  Official seal, light version for white paper and economical printing.

04-typography
  InstrumentSerif-Regular.ttf, InstrumentSerif-Italic.ttf   Primary typeface: school name, headings, tagline.
  InstrumentSans[wdth,wght].ttf                              Secondary typeface: descriptors, labels, body text.
  OFL.txt   Both typefaces are free to use under the SIL Open Font License.
  Install these fonts before opening the SVG files in Illustrator, Inkscape, or Canva,
  so that the lettering appears correctly.

06-3d
  renders-front/    Front-facing 3D renders of every logo type on a white background.
  renders-angled/   Three-quarter view renders of the seals, emblem badge, and primary logo.
  models/           3D models (.glb) of every logo type: the seals as gold-rimmed medallions,
                    the emblem as an arch badge, and the lockups as raised sign plaques.
                    Open them in Windows 3D Viewer, Blender (File > Import > glTF 2.0),
                    or any online glTF viewer such as gltf-viewer.donmccurdy.com.
                    The models suit presentations, mock-ups, and 3D printing previews;
                    the raised detail on each face is a surface (normal) map, not modelled geometry.

USAGE NOTES
  - Do not stretch, recolour, rotate, or add effects (shadows, glows, outlines) to the logos.
  - Keep clear space around each logo at least equal to the height of the emblem's sun.
  - Use the reversed or transparent versions on dark or photographic backgrounds.
